Observing Locomotion Patterns of Metazygia wilfradae in Light and Dark Conditions

Neuroscience 2023, Washington, D.C.

Analyzed locomotion and circadian rhythm data from 30 spiders across 14 days (10,000+ observations) in Python. Created visualizations with Matplotlib and Seaborn revealing circadian rhythms of 16–32 hours persisting independent of light exposure. 1 of 130 posters presented at Neuroscience 2023, Washington, D.C.
